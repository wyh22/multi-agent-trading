# Deep Research Skill

## Purpose
Execute complex multi-stage stock research tasks requiring multiple evidence sources.

## When To Use
- Comprehensive company research.
- Multi-source investment research reports.

## Workflow
1. Decompose the research objective.
2. Collect information from independent sources.
3. Validate evidence consistency.
4. Produce structured research output.

## Required Capabilities
- Market information
- Financial information
- News and disclosure retrieval
- RAG evidence retrieval

## Constraints
- Require evidence for important claims.
- Respect point-in-time research constraints.
- Do not generate trading decisions.
